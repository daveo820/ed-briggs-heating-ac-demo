# Design DNA: Ed Briggs Heating & A/C

- **Hero archetype:** dual split. Headline and facts on cream, then the screen splits into two full color halves, Heat (ember) and Cooling (frost), each with its own tap-to-call. New in the portfolio (none of the 13 earlier demos split the hero by job).
- **Signature motif:** the temperature bar, an ember to frost gradient. Under the header, logo mark base, subpage headers, the promise quote rule, top of the footer, and the phone call bar border (6 uses).
- **Tap-to-call:** header, both hero halves, oil furnace card, every service row, bands, footer sign, sticky call bar on phones.
- **Type:** Fraunces 600 (display, warm old-style serif that suits a 1960 company) plus Public Sans 400 to 700 (body, 18px). Both new to the portfolio, self hosted, preloaded.
- **Motion:** fade plus 20px rise once. Reduced motion shows all.

- **Shapes:** split hero; services ledger beside a navy oil and boiler card; full width quote band; review strip; service area split; footer closing sign. Rounded pill buttons (the earlier demos are mostly square).
